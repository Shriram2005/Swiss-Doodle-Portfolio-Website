SECTION // 01 ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Swiss Precision Meets Creative Hand-Drawn Warmth

I am Shriram Manikrao Mange, an Android Developer and Full-Stack Web Engineer based in Nashik, Maharashtra. I specialize in building performant mobile apps with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, as well as production-ready web apps with modern JavaScript and Python backends.

My design philosophy draws inspiration from the International Typographic Style (Swiss Style) — strict visual hierarchy, mathematical grids, and high contrast — tempered with the playful warmth of hand-drawn scribbles, doodles, and organic interactions. Technology shouldn’t feel cold or clinical; it should feel reliable, tactile, and human.

💡 Core Engineering Rules:
  • • No Unnecessary Complexity: State hoisting and unidirectional data flow (MVVM/MVI) first.
  • • Zero Hydration & Bundle Bloat: Performance is a core feature, not an afterthought.
  • • Tactile Feedback: Delight users with snappy micro-interactions and intentional motion.
